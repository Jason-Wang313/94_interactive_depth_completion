# Novelty Decision

Chosen thesis: Interactive Depth Completion tests whether a robot should physically probe where perception uncertainty matters to action.

New central mechanism tested: action-critical physical probing of uncertain depth cells.

Decision: KILL_ARCHIVE.

Reason: the v4 benchmark shows that the mechanism does not beat active view selection. The proposed method has lower combined-stress success, higher action-critical RMSE, higher collisions, higher regret, and nonzero probe damage. The novelty boundary is therefore not strong enough for ICLR main.
